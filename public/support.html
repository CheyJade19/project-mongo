Help and support

Household Calendar

Getting started

Sign in with Apple, Google or your email address, then start a household and add a profile for each person who lives with you. To share it, open Household, then Invite people, and send the code or link to each person. They sign in on their own phone or tablet and enter the code.

Adding events from email

Open an invite in your email app and choose Household Calendar, or tap From email in the app and paste the part of the email with the date. You can also forward one email to your private address (Household, then Email next to your name) and pick what to add from it. The app never reads your inbox.

An invite code doesn't work

Codes last 7 days and work for up to 10 people. Ask someone in the household to create a new one.

Leaving or deleting

Open Household, then Account and privacy. You can leave a household, delete it if you're the owner, sign out, or delete your account. See the privacy policy for what's removed.
