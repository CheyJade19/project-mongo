Privacy policy

Household Calendar · Last updated 14 October 2026

The short version. We store your household's calendar so everyone in it can see it. We never connect to or read your email inbox. There is no AI or automated reading of anything you send us. We don't sell data, show ads, or use trackers.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and name, and an identifier from Apple or Google if you sign in with them. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Your household: its name and time zone, the profiles you add (name, colour, optional email, and a profile photo if you upload one), events and to-dos, and who has access.
  • Signed-in devices: a scrambled (hashed) copy of each device's sign-in token, so we can tell it's you.
  • Emails you choose to forward (only if you use a forwarding address): see below.

Email: always your choice

The app never connects to your email account and never reads your inbox. There are three ways an email's details can reach the app, and you start each one yourself, one email at a time:

  1. Opening an invite (.ics) in your email app and choosing Household Calendar. The file is read on your device.
  2. Pasting part of an email, or choosing a saved email file, in the app. It is read on your device and isn't sent to us; only the events you choose to add are saved.
  3. Forwarding one email to your private household address. We keep the subject, sender, any calendar invites and a short excerpt so you can review it. Nothing is added to the calendar until you pick what to add. As soon as you add or dismiss it, the excerpt and invites are deleted. If you do neither, they're deleted after 14 days.

Dates are found with simple pattern matching (for example "Fri 21 October at 6pm"). No AI or machine learning reads your emails, and no email content is shared with anyone.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Cloudflare, which runs the service, and is sent over encrypted connections. If you sign in with an email code, the code is sent through our email provider, Resend. If you sign in with Apple or Google, they confirm who you are; we don't receive your password.

Who can see it

Only the people you invite into your household, who sign in with their own accounts. The household owner can remove people at any time. We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a profile, event, to-do or forwarded email at any time from the app.
  • Remove your profile photo under Profile. Photos are made smaller on your device before they're uploaded, are only shown inside your household, and are deleted when you remove or replace them.
  • Delete a household (owner): Account and privacy, then Delete household. Everything in it is removed for everyone.
  • Delete your account: Account and privacy, then Delete account. Your sign-in, devices and access are removed straight away. Households only you used are deleted with everything in them; a shared household passes to the next person in it.

Deleted data is removed from the live database immediately. Cloudflare's own backups may keep it for up to 30 days before it's gone for good.

Children

A parent or guardian can add children as profiles in their household. Children don't need their own account, and we don't knowingly collect accounts from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update the date above and mention it in the app. Questions or requests: support@example.com.
